# 💚 MediMate — AI-Powered Elderly Care Companion

**Take Care. Live Better.**

MediMate is an AI-powered healthcare application designed to help elderly people manage their daily medications and stay connected with their families.

The app provides smart medicine reminders, voice assistance, medication tracking, and caregiver notifications to help seniors maintain their medication routines while giving families greater peace of mind.

## ✨ Features

- 💊 **Smart Medicine Reminders** — Schedule and manage daily medications.
- 🔔 **Missed Dose Alerts** — Notify connected caregivers when a scheduled dose remains unconfirmed.
- 🎙️ **AI Voice Assistant** — Interact with MediMate using simple voice commands.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Family Care Dashboard** — Help caregivers monitor medication confirmation and daily progress.
- 📷 **Prescription Scanner** — Extract medicine information from prescription images for user verification.
- 📅 **Medication History** — Track scheduled, confirmed, and unconfirmed doses.
- 💧 **Daily Wellness Tracking** — Track basic wellness activities and daily routines.
- 🆘 **Emergency SOS** — Help users contact trusted family members during emergencies.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** — Designed with English, Hindi, and Telugu support in mind.
- ♿ **Senior-Friendly Interface** — Large buttons, readable text, simple navigation, and accessible design.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make healthcare management easier for elderly people and help families care for their loved ones, even when they are physically apart.

MediMate aims to reduce medication confusion, improve communication between patients and caregivers, and make everyday healthcare more accessible through technology.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Supabase
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Authentication: Supabase Auth
- AI: AI-powered assistant integration
- Notifications: Push notifications and scheduled reminders

## 🚀 Project Vision

MediMate aims to become a trusted digital healthcare companion for elderly people by combining medication management, AI assistance, and family connectivity in one simple application.

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

MediMate is a medication management and reminder application. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Users should follow their healthcare professional's instructions. MediMate does not independently prescribe medicines or recommend dosage changes.
